FOR THE ONES WHO KEEP DETROIT RUNNING

You worked the shift.
Now celebrate it.

Shiftsgiving is our thank-you to the people who make Detroit work. The UAW members on the line who build what the world drives. The nurses, doctors, techs and hospital staff who work around the clock to keep our families healthy. Early mornings, overnights, doubles and holidays: you show up every single shift to make life better for all of us. This night is for you.

The night before Thanksgiving is the biggest homecoming night of the year, and Me & You Kitchen + Cocktails is starting a new east side tradition. Clock out, bring your crew and let DJ Red and DJ Scratch take it from 5PM to 2AM. Day shift, night shift, double shift: everybody's welcome.
